Introduction

About me

My name is Max. I play football for FC Viktoria 1889 Berlin, currently in the U18 team in the A-Junioren Landesliga of Berlin. I occasionally start in running competitions for the club Silvesterlauf Trier. I started playing football around 2016, and in 2022/23 I made the Berlin state selection squad of my age group.

From 2023 to 2025 I lived in Dubai, where I joined Shabab Al Ahli, one of the biggest clubs in the UAE. I played in the UAE National League with the club's U15 and U16 first teams, alongside several UAE youth internationals. We finished runner-up four times in league, cup and super cup.

After returning to Germany in autumn 2025, I rejoined Viktoria Berlin and played the 2025/26 season with the U17 in the DFB U17 Nachwuchsliga. Since summer 2026 I have been playing in the U18 team. Check out the sites of my sisters Carla and Elena too.

Berlin · since autumn 2025

Shabab Al Ahli U16 & U15

Dubai, UAE · Sep 2023 – Jun 2025

Shabab Al Ahli is one of the most successful clubs in the UAE; its men's team plays in the ADNOC Pro League. I played for the U15 and U16 selection teams. My debut in the UAE National League U15 was on 26 December 2023: a 7:1 win against Hatta Dubai, and I scored.
